Restricted sections for crawling:
- /admin/
- /login/
- /private/

Specific rules for certain user agents:
- User-agent: Googlebot
  Disallow: /no-google/
- User-agent: *
  Disallow: /tmp/

Reflection:
Websites use robots.txt to control which parts of their site can be accessed by 
automated crawlers or bots. This helps protect sensitive or private content and 
prevents servers from being overloaded. Following robots.txt promotes ethical 
scraping by respecting the website owner’s rules.
